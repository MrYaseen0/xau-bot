# 🤖 XAU Bot — Gold Trading Dashboard

> An educational dashboard for XAU/USD (gold) traders: market snapshots, technical
> indicators, a position-size calculator, a strategy playbook, and a persistent
> paper-trading journal.

## ✨ Features

- **📊 Daily XAU/USD chart snapshots** — daily bars with source timestamps, plus
  technical indicators. Data arrives as a delayed daily snapshot, not live tick data.
- **🧮 Position-size calculator** — enter account balance, risk %, and stop-loss
  distance to get a sensible position size for the trade.
- **📚 Strategy playbook** — popular gold strategies written with clear rules
  (trend-following, breakout, range trading, and news caution).
- **📝 Paper-trading journal** — log simulated trades, track planned risk and
  realized paper P/L. Entries persist across sessions.

## ⚠️ Important — please read

- **Paper trading only.** Nothing here places real trades, connects to MT5/MT4,
  or touches a broker account. It is a practice and analysis tool.
- **Chart data is delayed** (daily snapshots with source timestamps). Always confirm
  live prices in your broker terminal before trading.
- **Educational, not financial advice.** Trading gold (XAU/USD) on leverage is
  high-risk — most retail traders lose money. Never trade money you cannot afford
  to lose.
- **No strategy guarantees profit** — including the ones in the playbook. Test
  everything on a demo account first.

## 🧠 How to use it well

1. Study the playbook and pick **one** strategy with rules you fully understand.
2. Use the calculator to size every paper trade (risking 1% or less per trade is a
   common starting point).
3. Journal every paper trade — entry, stop, target, outcome, and what you learned.
4. Review weekly. Only consider real capital after long, consistent demo results.
